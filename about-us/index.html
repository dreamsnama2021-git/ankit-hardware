Profile

Incorporated in the year 1986, we, “Ankit Hardware”, are a noted manufacturer of an exemplary array of System Window Accessories. In order to meet the varied industry requirements, we are offering Conventional Window Accessories and System Window (Domal Series) Accessories. All these products are engineered from our world-class in-house manufacturing facility. We manufactured these products with ultra-grade raw material sourced from the well-established vendors of the market. Our procuring agents conducted comprehensive market research before sourcing the raw material. Therefore, we have been able to manufacture the offered products as per the industry requirements. Advanced technologies are implemented for manufacturing these products so as to ensure their prolonged service life, high efficiency, and suitability for industrial as well as domestic applications. These products are acclaimed by the end users for their high strength, safety, perfect finish, corrosion resistance and various other special features.

Supported by a team of diligent professionals, we have been able to set up milestones in our domain. We facilitate our professionals all the advanced tools and flexibility to work in a highly productive environment. For this, we have established a state of the art infrastructure which is well-equipped with latest machines, tools, and technologies. It helps our professionals to manufacture the offered product line in accordance with the international quality standards. To ensure the quality, we tested these products on various parameters. Owing to the quality, our products prove to be highly valuable for the clients to meet their varied requirements. These products are delivered to the clients within given time-frame with the help of our diligent distribution network personnel. It helped us to save their time and achieve optimum client-satisfaction.

Under the able guidance of our mentor, “Mr. Jayantilal Shah”, we have been able to achieve our business goals within a very short period of time. He is highly experienced, talented and possesses detailed knowledge of every ins & outs of our business. Owing to his excellent managerial skills, we have been able to secure a reputed position in the market.
